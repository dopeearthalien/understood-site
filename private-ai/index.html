What it is

Your people ask questions in plain English and get answers drawn from your documents, your procedures and their own notes. Nothing else. If your material does not say it, the assistant says so, instead of guessing.

Every answer names the documents it read. It can also make a Word file, a PDF or a spreadsheet from what it found, in seconds.

It carries your name and your look, not ours. One members' association calls theirs The Oracle.

How it works

  1. 01
    Your material goes in.A shared folder, a knowledge base, a members' library. You choose what it may read. Nothing is copied anywhere else.
  2. 02
    Each person gets a login.Their own questions, their own notes, their own private space. Nobody sees anyone else's.
  3. 03
    They ask, it answers, it cites.Sources listed under every answer: your documents on one side, their notes on the other.

Only your sources. Nothing invented.

The one rule the whole system is built around: your organisation's material, and each person's own notes if you want them. Never the model's own ideas. It is tested every night: questions with known answers are put to it, and anything made up fails the test.

What it is not

It is not a general chatbot. Ask it about the weather and it will tell you that is not in your material. That is the point.

It is not a place your documents live. They stay where they are. The assistant reads them; it does not keep them.

It does not replace judgement. It finds, quotes and drafts, faster than a person can, and shows its working so a person can check.
